
An open-source RC-controlled mecanum buggy to drive a dog's bed.

## Why?

I had bought a set mecanum wheels a while ago off Aliexpress. Was fascinated by them but did not have a project in mind. 

Our dog is becoming increasingly old and lazy (aren't we all) so:
- figured she might like a Aladdin-like magic carpet style bed to float around on 
- really what to see her little pitbull face when her bed, the centre of her universe, starts moving

## FYI

You can buy a fairly comprehensive mecanum buggy set on Aliexpress to build a radio- controlled mecanum-wheeled vehicle at a fairly reasonable cost using DC motors with encoders.

I had enough parts in my parts bin to make a PS4 controller based buggy using stepper motors. And I wanted to learn about battery management and make my life (more) difficult*

(* No dog needs an RC-controlled bed. But all dog's would like one.)
